How to start, safely

5 steps · plain English
1

Get a self-custody wallet

A free app where you hold your own keys — you're the bank. Write down the recovery phrase and never share it.

2

Get some stablecoins

Buy USDC or USDT on a reputable exchange (or convert local currency), then send them to your wallet.

3

Pick a pool above

Start with a “Low” risk, large-TVL pool from a name you know — Aave, Compound or Sky are good first stops.

4

Open & deposit

Tap “Open”, connect your wallet on the protocol's own site, and deposit. Always double-check the web address.

5

Start small & watch

Try a small amount first. Star pools to your watchlist and check rates here. Never deposit more than you can lose.

New here? Start in plain English

01

What's a stablecoin?

A digital dollar. 1 USDT or USDC always aims to equal $1 — no wild swings like Bitcoin. Just dollars you can hold on your phone.

02

What's "yield"?

Interest. You lend dollars to a tested protocol and earn a yearly return — like a savings account, but the rates are usually higher.

03

How is this "safe"?

We only show big, established pools (over $10M deposited), sane rates, no risky pairing. Sky-high APYs are filtered out — usually traps.

04

Still your call

Crypto carries real risks — hacks, depegs, scams. This is a starting map, not advice. Open any pool to read more.

⚠Not financial advice. Stablecoins and DeFi carry real risks — smart-contract hacks, depegs and reward dumps happen. Inflation figures are rough estimates. Always do your own research before moving money.
